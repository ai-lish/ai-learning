1. Factorials and combinations1. 階乘與組合

Factorials階乘

  • For a positive integer \(n\), the product of the first \(n\) positive integers is \(n!\), read “n factorial”. [Oxford p. 2.2]對正整數 \(n\)，前 \(n\) 個正整數的乘積記作 \(n!\)，讀作「n 階乘」。[Oxford p. 2.2]
  • Definition: \(0!=1\). [Oxford p. 2.2]定義：\(0!=1\)。[Oxford p. 2.2]
  • Formula: \(n!=n(n-1)(n-2)\cdots3\cdot2\cdot1\); recurrence: \(n!=n(n-1)!\). [Oxford p. 2.2]公式：\(n!=n(n-1)(n-2)\cdots3\cdot2\cdot1\)；遞迴關係：\(n!=n(n-1)!\)。[Oxford p. 2.2]
  • Factorials of negative integers are undefined. [Oxford p. 2.2]負整數的階乘沒有定義。[Oxford p. 2.2]

Combinations組合

Choose \(r\) distinct objects from \(n\), without replacement and without regard to order. [Oxford pp. 2.3–2.4]從 \(n\) 個相異物件中不重複選取 \(r\) 個，且不計選取次序。[Oxford pp. 2.3–2.4]

\[C_r^n={}^{n}C_r=\frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!},\quad n,r\text{ are non-negative integers},\quad 0\le r\le n.\]

Special values: \(C_0^n=1\) and \(C_n^n=1\). [Oxford pp. 2.3–2.4]特殊值：\(C_0^n=1\) 及 \(C_n^n=1\)。[Oxford pp. 2.3–2.4]

Combination identities組合的性質

\[C_r^n=C_{n-r}^n,\qquad C_r^n+C_{r+1}^n=C_{r+1}^{n+1}\quad(0\le r<n).\]

Derivation hint: the symmetry follows by cancelling common factors in the factorial formula. For Pascal’s identity, use a common denominator, with \((r+1)!=(r+1)r!\) and \((n-r)!=(n-r)(n-r-1)!\). [Oxford p. 2.6] [需教材校對]推導提示：對稱性可由階乘公式約去相同因子；帕斯卡恆等式可通分，並使用 \((r+1)!=(r+1)r!\) 及 \((n-r)!=(n-r)(n-r-1)!\)。[Oxford p. 2.6] [需教材校對]

2. Pascal’s triangle2. 帕斯卡三角形

  • Row \(n\) has \(n+1\) entries. The two end entries are 1; each row is symmetric. [Oxford pp. 2.8–2.9]第 \(n\) 行有 \(n+1\) 個數；兩端皆為 1，而且每行左右對稱。[Oxford pp. 2.8–2.9]
  • Each interior entry is the sum of the two entries immediately above it. The entries can be written as \(C_r^n\). [Oxford p. 2.9]每個內部數等於正上方兩數之和；各數可寫成 \(C_r^n\)。[Oxford p. 2.9]
  • The coefficient rows of \((a+b)^0,(a+b)^1,(a+b)^2,\ldots\) begin \(1;\ 1,1;\ 1,2,1;\ldots\). [Oxford p. 2.8]\((a+b)^0,(a+b)^1,(a+b)^2,\ldots\) 的係數行依次為 \(1;\ 1,1;\ 1,2,1;\ldots\)。[Oxford p. 2.8]
  • In a coefficient row for \((a+b)^n\), there are \(n+1\) entries, each \(C_r^n\). As the term number increases, the exponent of \(a\) decreases by 1 and the exponent of \(b\) increases by 1. [Oxford pp. 2.8–2.10] [需教材校對]\((a+b)^n\) 的係數行有 \(n+1\) 個數，各數為 \(C_r^n\)；項次增加時，\(a\) 的指數逐項減 1，\(b\) 的指數逐項加 1。[Oxford pp. 2.8–2.10] [需教材校對]
  • The triangle is also known as Yang Hui’s triangle, after a 13th-century record in 《詳解九章算法》. Constructing rows becomes cumbersome when \(n\) is large. [Oxford pp. 2.9–2.10] [需教材校對]13 世紀《詳解九章算法》已有相關記載，因此亦稱「楊輝三角」；當 \(n\) 很大時，逐行尋找係數會很繁瑣。[Oxford pp. 2.9–2.10] [需教材校對]

3. Binomial Theorem and applications3. 二項式定理及其應用

The Binomial Theorem二項式定理

A polynomial with two terms is a binomial, for example \(1+x\), \(a+b\), or \(2x-3y\). [Oxford p. 2.8]只有兩項的多項式稱為二項式，例如 \(1+x\)、\(a+b\) 或 \(2x-3y\)。[Oxford p. 2.8]

In the \(n\) brackets of \((a+b)^n\), choosing \(r\) copies of \(b\) and \(n-r\) copies of \(a\) gives \(a^{n-r}b^r\), in \(C_r^n\) ways. [Oxford pp. 2.10–2.11]在 \((a+b)^n\) 的 \(n\) 個括號中選取 \(r\) 個 \(b\) 及 \(n-r\) 個 \(a\)，會得到 \(a^{n-r}b^r\)，共有 \(C_r^n\) 種選法。[Oxford pp. 2.10–2.11]

For a positive integer \(n\), \(0\le r\le n\), and real \(a,b\): [Oxford p. 2.11]若 \(n\) 為正整數、\(0\le r\le n\)，且 \(a,b\) 為實數：[Oxford p. 2.11]

\[(a+b)^n=\sum_{r=0}^{n}C_r^n a^{n-r}b^r\]
\[=C_0^n a^n+C_1^n a^{n-1}b+\cdots+C_n^n b^n.\]

There are \(n+1\) terms. In each term, the exponents of \(a\) and \(b\) sum to \(n\); in descending powers of \(a\), its exponent decreases by 1 while that of \(b\) increases by 1. [Oxford pp. 2.8, 2.11] [需教材校對]展開式共有 \(n+1\) 項；每項中 \(a,b\) 的指數和為 \(n\)；按 \(a\) 降冪排列時，\(a\) 的指數逐項減 1，\(b\) 的指數逐項加 1。[Oxford pp. 2.8, 2.11] [需教材校對]

Special case: \((1+x)^n=\sum_{r=0}^{n}C_r^n x^r\). [Oxford p. 2.11]特例：\((1+x)^n=\sum_{r=0}^{n}C_r^n x^r\)。[Oxford p. 2.11]

General term, when arranged in descending powers of \(a\) (or ascending powers of \(b\)): [Oxford pp. 2.11, 2.13]按 \(a\) 降冪（或 \(b\) 升冪）排列時的通項：[Oxford pp. 2.11, 2.13]

\[T_{r+1}=C_r^n a^{n-r}b^r.\]

The coefficient of \(a^{n-r}b^r\) is \(C_r^n\). For the \(k\)th term, set \(r=k-1\). [Oxford pp. 2.11, 2.14]\((a^{n-r}b^r)\) 的係數為 \(C_r^n\)；求第 \(k\) 項時，取 \(r=k-1\)。[Oxford pp. 2.11, 2.14]

The source confirms \(n+1\) terms but does not give a standalone formula for the middle term(s). [需教材校對]來源確認展開式有 \(n+1\) 項，但沒有提供獨立的中間項公式。[需教材校對]

Useful coefficients: \(C_1^n=n\), \(C_2^n=\frac{n(n-1)}2\), \(C_3^n=\frac{n(n-1)(n-2)}6\). [Oxford p. 2.17]常用組合數：\(C_1^n=n\)、\(C_2^n=\frac{n(n-1)}2\)、\(C_3^n=\frac{n(n-1)(n-2)}6\)。[Oxford p. 2.17]

Induction proof outline數學歸納法證明要點

In the induction step, multiply the \(k\)th expansion by \((a+b)\), collect like powers, and use \(C(k,r)+C(k,r-1)=C(k+1,r)\) for interior coefficients. The two end coefficients remain 1. [Oxford p. 2.12] [需教材校對]歸納步驟把第 \(k\) 次展開式乘以 \((a+b)\)，合併同類項，對內部係數使用帕斯卡恆等式 \(C(k,r)+C(k,r-1)=C(k+1,r)\)；兩端係數仍為 1。[Oxford p. 2.12] [需教材校對]

Common mistakes常見錯誤

  • Do not combine factorials across addition: \(4!+3!=30\), not \(7!\). [Oxford p. 2.2]不可把加法中的階乘直接合併：\(4!+3!=30\)，不等於 \(7!\)。[Oxford p. 2.2]
  • Do not use \(n!\) for a negative integer, and do not replace \(0!\) with 0. [Oxford p. 2.2]負整數的階乘沒有定義；亦不可把 \(0!\) 寫成 0。[Oxford p. 2.2]
  • Signs alternate in an expansion involving a negative term. [Oxford pp. 2.12–2.13]展開式含負項時，要留意各項正負號交替。[Oxford pp. 2.12–2.13]
  • For a constant term, set the exponent of the variable to 0. [Oxford p. 2.14] [需教材校對]求常數項時，令未知數的指數等於 0。[Oxford p. 2.14] [需教材校對]
  • For the \(k\)th term, use \(r=k-1\), not \(r=k\). [Oxford p. 2.14]求第 \(k\) 項時取 \(r=k-1\)，不是 \(r=k\)。[Oxford p. 2.14]
  • For a partial expansion, retain the required \(+\cdots\). [Oxford p. 2.16]只展開至指定項數時，要保留所需的 \(+\cdots\)。[Oxford p. 2.16]

Quick formula sheet公式速查

\[n!=n(n-1)\cdots2\cdot1,\quad0!=1.\]
\[C_r^n=\frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!},\quad C_r^n=C_{n-r}^n.\]
\[C_r^n+C_{r+1}^n=C_{r+1}^{n+1}.\]
\[(a+b)^n=\sum_{r=0}^{n}C_r^n a^{n-r}b^r,\quad T_{r+1}=C_r^n a^{n-r}b^r.\]
