I. Radian measureI. 弧度制

A. Radians and arc lengthA. 弧度與弧長

  • Definition: if arc \(AB\) has length equal to radius \(OA=r\), the angle it subtends at the centre is one radian. [Oxford p. 3.2]定義：若弧 \(AB\) 的長度等於半徑 \(OA=r\)，該弧所對的圓心角為 1 弧度。[Oxford p. 3.2]
  • For the same circle, \(\frac{\theta}{1\text{ rad}}=\frac{\text{arc }BC}{\text{arc }AB}\). If the arc length is \(l\), then \(\theta=\frac lr\text{ rad}\), so \(l=r\theta\) when \(\theta\) is measured in radians. [Oxford p. 3.3]同一圓內，\(\frac{\theta}{1\text{ rad}}=\frac{\text{弧 }BC}{\text{弧 }AB}\)。若弧長為 \(l\)，則 \(\theta=\frac lr\text{ rad}\)，亦即 \(l=r\theta\)（\(\theta\) 以弧度計）。[Oxford p. 3.3]
  • Conversion: \(180^\circ=\pi\text{ rad}\); \(1^\circ=\frac{\pi}{180}\text{ rad}\approx0.0175\text{ rad}\); \(1\text{ rad}=(\frac{180}{\pi})^\circ\approx57.3^\circ\). [Oxford pp. 3.4–3.5]換算：\(180^\circ=\pi\text{ rad}\)；\(1^\circ=\frac{\pi}{180}\text{ rad}\approx0.0175\text{ rad}\)；\(1\text{ rad}=(\frac{180}{\pi})^\circ\approx57.3^\circ\)。[Oxford pp. 3.4–3.5]
  • When the context is clear, the unit rad may be omitted. [Oxford p. 3.2]情境清楚時可省略 rad 單位。[Oxford p. 3.2]

II. The six trigonometric functionsII. 六個三角函數

A. Terminal-side coordinate definitionA. 終邊座標定義

  • Let \(P(x,y)\) lie on the terminal side of \(\theta\), with \(OP=r=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}>0\). The definition also applies to negative angles. [Oxford p. 3.9]設 \(P(x,y)\) 位於 \(\theta\) 的終邊，\(OP=r=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}>0\)。定義亦適用於負角。[Oxford p. 3.9]
  • \(\sin\theta=\frac yr\); \(\operatorname{cosec}\theta=\frac1{\sin\theta}=\frac ry\), where \(y\ne0\). [Oxford p. 3.8]\(\sin\theta=\frac yr\)；\(\operatorname{cosec}\theta=\frac1{\sin\theta}=\frac ry\)，其中 \(y\ne0\)。[Oxford p. 3.8]
  • \(\cos\theta=\frac xr\); \(\sec\theta=\frac1{\cos\theta}=\frac rx\), where \(x\ne0\). [Oxford p. 3.8]\(\cos\theta=\frac xr\)；\(\sec\theta=\frac1{\cos\theta}=\frac rx\)，其中 \(x\ne0\)。[Oxford p. 3.8]
  • For \(x\ne0\), \(\tan\theta=\frac yx\); for \(y\ne0\), \(\cot\theta=\frac xy\); when both \(x\ne0\) and \(y\ne0\), \(\cot\theta=\frac1{\tan\theta}\). [Oxford p. 3.8]當 \(x\ne0\)，\(\tan\theta=\frac yx\)；當 \(y\ne0\)，\(\cot\theta=\frac xy\)；當 \(x\ne0\) 且 \(y\ne0\)，\(\cot\theta=\frac1{\tan\theta}\)。[Oxford p. 3.8]
  • Secant, cosecant and cotangent are reciprocal functions. Do not confuse them with inverse-function calculator keys; check the angle unit. [Oxford pp. 3.8–3.11]sec、cosec、cot 為倒數函數；勿與計算機反函數按鍵混淆，並核對角度單位。[Oxford pp. 3.8–3.11]

B. Values and signs by quadrantB. 象限值及符號

  • At \(\theta=0,\frac\pi2,\pi,\frac{3\pi}2,2\pi\): \(\sin\theta=0,1,0,-1,0\); \(\operatorname{cosec}\theta\) is undefined, \(1\), undefined, \(-1\), undefined. [Oxford p. 3.10]\(\theta=0,\frac\pi2,\pi,\frac{3\pi}2,2\pi\) 時：\(\sin\theta=0,1,0,-1,0\)；\(\operatorname{cosec}\theta\) 依次為未定義、\(1\)、未定義、\(-1\)、未定義。[Oxford p. 3.10]
  • At those angles: \(\cos\theta=1,0,-1,0,1\); \(\sec\theta=1\), undefined, \(-1\), undefined, \(1\). [Oxford p. 3.10]上述角度的 \(\cos\theta=1,0,-1,0,1\)；\(\sec\theta\) 依次為 \(1\)、未定義、\(-1\)、未定義、\(1\)。[Oxford p. 3.10]
  • At those angles: \(\tan\theta=0\), undefined, \(0\), undefined, \(0\); \(\cot\theta\) is undefined, \(0\), undefined, \(0\), undefined. [Oxford p. 3.10]上述角度的 \(\tan\theta\) 依次為 \(0\)、未定義、\(0\)、未定義、\(0\)；\(\cot\theta\) 依次為未定義、\(0\)、未定義、\(0\)、未定義。[Oxford p. 3.10]
  • CAST: all six functions are positive in quadrant I; only sine and cosecant in II; only tangent and cotangent in III; only cosine and secant in IV. [Oxford p. 3.12]CAST：第一象限六個函數均正；第二象限僅 sin、cosec 正；第三象限僅 tan、cot 正；第四象限僅 cos、sec 正。[Oxford p. 3.12]
  • Use the signs of \(x\) and \(y\) from the coordinate definition to determine the quadrant signs. [Oxford p. 3.12]按座標定義使用 \(x\)、\(y\) 的象限正負號。[Oxford p. 3.12]

III. Trigonometric identitiesIII. 三角恆等式

A. Basic identitiesA. 基本恆等式

  • Reciprocal identities: \(\operatorname{cosec}\theta=\frac1{\sin\theta}\); \(\sec\theta=\frac1{\cos\theta}\); \(\cot\theta=\frac1{\tan\theta}\). [Oxford p. 3.20]倒數恆等式：\(\operatorname{cosec}\theta=\frac1{\sin\theta}\)；\(\sec\theta=\frac1{\cos\theta}\)；\(\cot\theta=\frac1{\tan\theta}\)。[Oxford p. 3.20]
  • Quotient identities: \(\tan\theta=\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}\); \(\cot\theta=\frac{\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}\). [Oxford p. 3.21]商式恆等式：\(\tan\theta=\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}\)；\(\cot\theta=\frac{\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}\)。[Oxford p. 3.21]
  • Pythagorean identity: \(\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1\). [Oxford p. 3.21]畢氏恆等式：\(\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1\)。[Oxford p. 3.21]
  • Derived identities: \(1+\tan^2\theta=\sec^2\theta\), where \(\cos\theta\ne0\); \(1+\cot^2\theta=\operatorname{cosec}^2\theta\), where \(\sin\theta\ne0\). [Oxford p. 3.21]推得恆等式：\(1+\tan^2\theta=\sec^2\theta\)（\(\cos\theta\ne0\)）；\(1+\cot^2\theta=\operatorname{cosec}^2\theta\)（\(\sin\theta\ne0\)）。[Oxford p. 3.21]

B. Related-angle formulaeB. 關聯角公式

  • Negative angles: \(\sin(-\theta)=-\sin\theta\); \(\operatorname{cosec}(-\theta)=-\operatorname{cosec}\theta\); \(\cos(-\theta)=\cos\theta\); \(\sec(-\theta)=\sec\theta\); \(\tan(-\theta)=-\tan\theta\); \(\cot(-\theta)=-\cot\theta\). [Oxford pp. 3.23–3.24]負角：\(\sin(-\theta)=-\sin\theta\)；\(\operatorname{cosec}(-\theta)=-\operatorname{cosec}\theta\)；\(\cos(-\theta)=\cos\theta\)；\(\sec(-\theta)=\sec\theta\)；\(\tan(-\theta)=-\tan\theta\)；\(\cot(-\theta)=-\cot\theta\)。[Oxford pp. 3.23–3.24]
  • At \(180^\circ-\theta\): \(\sin(180^\circ-\theta)=\sin\theta\); \(\operatorname{cosec}(180^\circ-\theta)=\operatorname{cosec}\theta\); \(\cos(180^\circ-\theta)=-\cos\theta\); \(\sec(180^\circ-\theta)=-\sec\theta\); \(\tan(180^\circ-\theta)=-\tan\theta\); \(\cot(180^\circ-\theta)=-\cot\theta\). [Oxford pp. 3.23–3.24]\(180^\circ-\theta\)：\(\sin(180^\circ-\theta)=\sin\theta\)；\(\operatorname{cosec}(180^\circ-\theta)=\operatorname{cosec}\theta\)；\(\cos(180^\circ-\theta)=-\cos\theta\)；\(\sec(180^\circ-\theta)=-\sec\theta\)；\(\tan(180^\circ-\theta)=-\tan\theta\)；\(\cot(180^\circ-\theta)=-\cot\theta\)。[Oxford pp. 3.23–3.24]
  • At \(180^\circ+\theta\): \(\sin(180^\circ+\theta)=-\sin\theta\); \(\operatorname{cosec}(180^\circ+\theta)=-\operatorname{cosec}\theta\); \(\cos(180^\circ+\theta)=-\cos\theta\); \(\sec(180^\circ+\theta)=-\sec\theta\); \(\tan(180^\circ+\theta)=\tan\theta\); \(\cot(180^\circ+\theta)=\cot\theta\). [Oxford pp. 3.23–3.24]\(180^\circ+\theta\)：\(\sin(180^\circ+\theta)=-\sin\theta\)；\(\operatorname{cosec}(180^\circ+\theta)=-\operatorname{cosec}\theta\)；\(\cos(180^\circ+\theta)=-\cos\theta\)；\(\sec(180^\circ+\theta)=-\sec\theta\)；\(\tan(180^\circ+\theta)=\tan\theta\)；\(\cot(180^\circ+\theta)=\cot\theta\)。[Oxford pp. 3.23–3.24]
  • At \(360^\circ-\theta\): \(\sin(360^\circ-\theta)=-\sin\theta\); \(\operatorname{cosec}(360^\circ-\theta)=-\operatorname{cosec}\theta\); \(\cos(360^\circ-\theta)=\cos\theta\); \(\sec(360^\circ-\theta)=\sec\theta\); \(\tan(360^\circ-\theta)=-\tan\theta\); \(\cot(360^\circ-\theta)=-\cot\theta\). [Oxford pp. 3.23–3.24]\(360^\circ-\theta\)：\(\sin(360^\circ-\theta)=-\sin\theta\)；\(\operatorname{cosec}(360^\circ-\theta)=-\operatorname{cosec}\theta\)；\(\cos(360^\circ-\theta)=\cos\theta\)；\(\sec(360^\circ-\theta)=\sec\theta\)；\(\tan(360^\circ-\theta)=-\tan\theta\)；\(\cot(360^\circ-\theta)=-\cot\theta\)。[Oxford pp. 3.23–3.24]
  • At \(360^\circ+\theta\): \(\sin(360^\circ+\theta)=\sin\theta\); \(\operatorname{cosec}(360^\circ+\theta)=\operatorname{cosec}\theta\); \(\cos(360^\circ+\theta)=\cos\theta\); \(\sec(360^\circ+\theta)=\sec\theta\); \(\tan(360^\circ+\theta)=\tan\theta\); \(\cot(360^\circ+\theta)=\cot\theta\). [Oxford pp. 3.23–3.24]\(360^\circ+\theta\)：六個函數均重複：\(\sin(360^\circ+\theta)=\sin\theta\)；\(\operatorname{cosec}(360^\circ+\theta)=\operatorname{cosec}\theta\)；\(\cos(360^\circ+\theta)=\cos\theta\)；\(\sec(360^\circ+\theta)=\sec\theta\)；\(\tan(360^\circ+\theta)=\tan\theta\)；\(\cot(360^\circ+\theta)=\cot\theta\)。[Oxford pp. 3.23–3.24]
  • At \(90^\circ-\theta\): \(\sin(90^\circ-\theta)=\cos\theta\); \(\operatorname{cosec}(90^\circ-\theta)=\sec\theta\); \(\cos(90^\circ-\theta)=\sin\theta\); \(\sec(90^\circ-\theta)=\operatorname{cosec}\theta\); \(\tan(90^\circ-\theta)=\cot\theta\); \(\cot(90^\circ-\theta)=\tan\theta\). [Oxford pp. 3.23–3.24]\(90^\circ-\theta\)：\(\sin(90^\circ-\theta)=\cos\theta\)；\(\operatorname{cosec}(90^\circ-\theta)=\sec\theta\)；\(\cos(90^\circ-\theta)=\sin\theta\)；\(\sec(90^\circ-\theta)=\operatorname{cosec}\theta\)；\(\tan(90^\circ-\theta)=\cot\theta\)；\(\cot(90^\circ-\theta)=\tan\theta\)。[Oxford pp. 3.23–3.24]
  • At \(90^\circ+\theta\): \(\sin(90^\circ+\theta)=\cos\theta\); \(\operatorname{cosec}(90^\circ+\theta)=\sec\theta\); \(\cos(90^\circ+\theta)=-\sin\theta\); \(\sec(90^\circ+\theta)=-\operatorname{cosec}\theta\); \(\tan(90^\circ+\theta)=-\cot\theta\); \(\cot(90^\circ+\theta)=-\tan\theta\). [Oxford pp. 3.23–3.24]\(90^\circ+\theta\)：\(\sin(90^\circ+\theta)=\cos\theta\)；\(\operatorname{cosec}(90^\circ+\theta)=\sec\theta\)；\(\cos(90^\circ+\theta)=-\sin\theta\)；\(\sec(90^\circ+\theta)=-\operatorname{cosec}\theta\)；\(\tan(90^\circ+\theta)=-\cot\theta\)；\(\cot(90^\circ+\theta)=-\tan\theta\)。[Oxford pp. 3.23–3.24]
  • At \(270^\circ-\theta\): \(\sin(270^\circ-\theta)=-\cos\theta\); \(\operatorname{cosec}(270^\circ-\theta)=-\sec\theta\); \(\cos(270^\circ-\theta)=-\sin\theta\); \(\sec(270^\circ-\theta)=-\operatorname{cosec}\theta\); \(\tan(270^\circ-\theta)=\cot\theta\); \(\cot(270^\circ-\theta)=\tan\theta\). [Oxford pp. 3.23–3.24]\(270^\circ-\theta\)：\(\sin(270^\circ-\theta)=-\cos\theta\)；\(\operatorname{cosec}(270^\circ-\theta)=-\sec\theta\)；\(\cos(270^\circ-\theta)=-\sin\theta\)；\(\sec(270^\circ-\theta)=-\operatorname{cosec}\theta\)；\(\tan(270^\circ-\theta)=\cot\theta\)；\(\cot(270^\circ-\theta)=\tan\theta\)。[Oxford pp. 3.23–3.24]
  • At \(270^\circ+\theta\): \(\sin(270^\circ+\theta)=-\cos\theta\); \(\operatorname{cosec}(270^\circ+\theta)=-\sec\theta\); \(\cos(270^\circ+\theta)=\sin\theta\); \(\sec(270^\circ+\theta)=\operatorname{cosec}\theta\); \(\tan(270^\circ+\theta)=-\cot\theta\); \(\cot(270^\circ+\theta)=-\tan\theta\). [Oxford pp. 3.23–3.24]\(270^\circ+\theta\)：\(\sin(270^\circ+\theta)=-\cos\theta\)；\(\operatorname{cosec}(270^\circ+\theta)=-\sec\theta\)；\(\cos(270^\circ+\theta)=\sin\theta\)；\(\sec(270^\circ+\theta)=\operatorname{cosec}\theta\)；\(\tan(270^\circ+\theta)=-\cot\theta\)；\(\cot(270^\circ+\theta)=-\tan\theta\)。[Oxford pp. 3.23–3.24]

IV. Compound-angle formulaeIV. 複角公式

A. Sine and cosineA. 正弦及餘弦

  • \(\sin(A+B)=\sin A\cos B+\cos A\sin B\). [Oxford pp. 3.31–3.32]\(\sin(A+B)=\sin A\cos B+\cos A\sin B\)。[Oxford pp. 3.31–3.32]
  • \(\sin(A-B)=\sin A\cos B-\cos A\sin B\). [Oxford pp. 3.31–3.32]\(\sin(A-B)=\sin A\cos B-\cos A\sin B\)。[Oxford pp. 3.31–3.32]
  • \(\cos(A+B)=\cos A\cos B-\sin A\sin B\); \(\cos(A-B)=\cos A\cos B+\sin A\sin B\). [Oxford pp. 3.31–3.32]\(\cos(A+B)=\cos A\cos B-\sin A\sin B\)；\(\cos(A-B)=\cos A\cos B+\sin A\sin B\)。[Oxford pp. 3.31–3.32]
  • Do not split \(\sin(A+B)\) or \(\cos(A+B)\) into separate function values; the \(\sin A\sin B\) terms have opposite signs in the two cosine formulae. [Oxford pp. 3.31–3.32]不可把 \(\sin(A+B)\)、\(\cos(A+B)\) 拆成各自函數值的和；兩條餘弦公式中的 \(\sin A\sin B\) 項符號相反。[Oxford pp. 3.31–3.32]

B. TangentB. 正切

  • \(\tan(A+B)=\frac{\tan A+\tan B}{1-\tan A\tan B}\); \(\tan(A-B)=\frac{\tan A-\tan B}{1+\tan A\tan B}\). [Oxford p. 3.32]\(\tan(A+B)=\frac{\tan A+\tan B}{1-\tan A\tan B}\)；\(\tan(A-B)=\frac{\tan A-\tan B}{1+\tan A\tan B}\)。[Oxford p. 3.32]

V. Double-angle formulaeV. 二倍角公式

A. Sine, cosine and tangentA. 正弦、餘弦及正切

  • \(\sin2A=2\sin A\cos A\). [Oxford p. 3.40]\(\sin2A=2\sin A\cos A\)。[Oxford p. 3.40]
  • \(\cos2A=\cos^2A-\sin^2A=2\cos^2A-1=1-2\sin^2A\). [Oxford p. 3.40]\(\cos2A=\cos^2A-\sin^2A=2\cos^2A-1=1-2\sin^2A\)。[Oxford p. 3.40]
  • \(\tan2A=\frac{2\tan A}{1-\tan^2A}\). [Oxford p. 3.40]\(\tan2A=\frac{2\tan A}{1-\tan^2A}\)。[Oxford p. 3.40]

B. Power-reduction formulaeB. 降冪公式

  • \(\sin^2A=\frac12(1-\cos2A)\); \(\cos^2A=\frac12(1+\cos2A)\). [Oxford p. 3.40]\(\sin^2A=\frac12(1-\cos2A)\)；\(\cos^2A=\frac12(1+\cos2A)\)。[Oxford p. 3.40]
  • Before taking square roots in an equation, determine possible signs from the angle range or quadrant. [Oxford p. 3.42]方程開平方前，先按角度範圍或象限判定可能的正負號。[Oxford p. 3.42]

VI. Sum and product formulaeVI. 和積互化公式

A. Product-to-sumA. 積化和差

  • \(2\sin A\cos B=\sin(A+B)+\sin(A-B)\). [Oxford p. 3.48]\(2\sin A\cos B=\sin(A+B)+\sin(A-B)\)。[Oxford p. 3.48]
  • \(2\cos A\cos B=\cos(A+B)+\cos(A-B)\). [Oxford p. 3.48]\(2\cos A\cos B=\cos(A+B)+\cos(A-B)\)。[Oxford p. 3.48]
  • \(2\sin A\sin B=\cos(A-B)-\cos(A+B)\). [Oxford p. 3.48]\(2\sin A\sin B=\cos(A-B)-\cos(A+B)\)。[Oxford p. 3.48]
  • \(2\cos A\sin B=\sin(A+B)-\sin(A-B)\). [Oxford p. 3.48]\(2\cos A\sin B=\sin(A+B)-\sin(A-B)\)。[Oxford p. 3.48]

B. Sum-to-productB. 和差化積

  • \(\sin A+\sin B=2\sin\frac{A+B}{2}\cos\frac{A-B}{2}\). [Oxford p. 3.50]\(\sin A+\sin B=2\sin\frac{A+B}{2}\cos\frac{A-B}{2}\)。[Oxford p. 3.50]
  • \(\sin A-\sin B=2\cos\frac{A+B}{2}\sin\frac{A-B}{2}\). [Oxford p. 3.50]\(\sin A-\sin B=2\cos\frac{A+B}{2}\sin\frac{A-B}{2}\)。[Oxford p. 3.50]
  • \(\cos A+\cos B=2\cos\frac{A+B}{2}\cos\frac{A-B}{2}\). [Oxford p. 3.50]\(\cos A+\cos B=2\cos\frac{A+B}{2}\cos\frac{A-B}{2}\)。[Oxford p. 3.50]
  • \(\cos A-\cos B=-2\sin\frac{A+B}{2}\sin\frac{A-B}{2}\). [Oxford p. 3.50]\(\cos A-\cos B=-2\sin\frac{A+B}{2}\sin\frac{A-B}{2}\)。[Oxford p. 3.50]

Common mistakes常見錯誤

Common mistakes常見錯誤

  • Use radians in \(l=r\theta\); omit rad only when the context is clear. [Oxford pp. 3.2–3.3]運用 \(l=r\theta\) 時，\(\theta\) 以弧度計；情境清楚時才省略 rad。[Oxford pp. 3.2–3.3]
  • Check \(x\) and \(y\) signs by quadrant before evaluating a function. [Oxford p. 3.12]求值前先按象限核對 \(x\)、\(y\) 的正負號。[Oxford p. 3.12]
  • Do not confuse reciprocal functions with inverse-function calculator keys; check degrees or radians. [Oxford pp. 3.8–3.11]勿把倒數函數與計算機反函數混淆；核對角度使用度數還是弧度。[Oxford pp. 3.8–3.11]
  • Do not split a compound sine or cosine into separate function values; check the sign of \(\sin A\sin B\) in the cosine formulae. [Oxford pp. 3.31–3.32]不可把複角正弦或餘弦拆開；核對餘弦公式中 \(\sin A\sin B\) 項的正負號。[Oxford pp. 3.31–3.32]
  • Before taking a square root, use the angle range or quadrant to choose the possible sign. [Oxford p. 3.42]開平方前先按角度範圍或象限判斷正負號。[Oxford p. 3.42]
