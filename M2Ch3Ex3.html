Assumption:約定： In symbolic questions, $0^\circ<\theta<90^\circ$. Reference-angle questions keep their given numerical angle.符號題均設 $0^\circ<\theta<90^\circ$；參考角題保留題目給定的數值角。

Coverage:範圍： Level 1 uses eight angle forms from $-\theta$ through $360^\circ-\theta$. Level 2 uses seven forms above one full turn or written as $\theta-90^\circ k$.程度一包括 $-\theta$ 至 $360^\circ-\theta$ 的八種角型；程度二包括超過一整圈的四種角型及 $\theta-90^\circ k$ 三種角型。
