What changes?每級有甚麼變化？

The bounds change, but the rule is always: include every integer from the lower bound to the upper bound.上下界會改變，但規則一直是：由下界到上界，包含每一個整數。

  • 1. Fixed lower and upper numbers.固定數值上下界。
  • 2. The upper bound is n.上界是 n。
  • 3. Both bounds move with n.上下界都隨 n 移動。
